Privacy Policy for OpenRelay VPN

Last updated: September 2026

OpenRelay VPN ("we", "our", or "the application") is committed to respecting and safeguarding user privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines our principles and practices regarding information handling.

1. No Personal Data Collection

OpenRelay VPN does NOT collect, store, track, sell, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII).

  • No User Accounts: No sign-up, registration, email address, password, or profile information is ever collected.
  • No Financial Data: The application is 100% free; no payment details or billing data are processed.
  • No Analytics or Trackers: The application contains zero third-party advertising SDKs, trackers, or behavioral analytics services.

2. Use of Android VpnService

OpenRelay VPN utilizes Android's official VpnService API to establish virtual private network tunnels (via OpenVPN and WireGuard protocols).

  • Encryption: All Internet traffic transmitted while the VPN tunnel is active is encrypted to protect your connection against eavesdropping on public Wi-Fi networks.
  • No Client-side Logging: The application itself never inspects, captures, logs, or stores your browsing history, visited domain names, web traffic payloads, or DNS lookups.

3. Third-Party Relay Servers

OpenRelay VPN connects to public, volunteer-operated relay servers or public Anycast relays. OpenRelay VPN is an independent open-source client. Volunteer servers operated by independent third parties may maintain operational connection logs according to their own server configurations.

4. Local Preferences

Any settings configured in the app (such as dark/light theme mode, selected UI language, and acceptance of the prominent disclosure dialog) are stored exclusively on your device using local storage (SharedPreferences). These settings never leave your device.

5. Open Source Transparency & License

OpenRelay VPN is free and open-source software. The source code is publicly inspectable on GitHub: github.com/KOUSSEMON-Aurel/vpngate.

6. Contact & Support

If you have any questions, feedback, or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please open an issue on our official GitHub repository.
